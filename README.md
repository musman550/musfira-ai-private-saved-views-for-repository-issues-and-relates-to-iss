## Overview

**Today, GitHub is introducing a new feature that enhances the way we manage and access our repositories. By providing a private saved view for repository issues, GitHub users can create and save their own unique views of issues within a repository. This feature not only enhances the functionality of the platform but also provides a more personalized experience for developers.

Imagine a developer named Jane, who frequently works on a large project with several branches. She finds herself frequently revisiting the same issues and updates across multiple branches. Previously, she had to manually create and manage views for each branch, which was time-consuming and prone to errors. Now, with the private saved view feature, Jane can save a private view of the issues related to her project. This view will automatically update with the latest information and can be accessed whenever she needs it, regardless of her current location or the branches she's working on. This feature makes her workflow more efficient and reduces the likelihood of missing important updates.

## Key Features

Five Descriptions of One Capability

**1. Private Saved Views for Repository Issues:** A user can create and save their own views of issues within a repository, ensuring that the information is only accessible to them.

**2. Issue Relationship Support:** Now, when a user adds an issue to a “Relates to” relationship, they can see the related issues in their saved view, making it easier to follow the chain of related issues.

**3. Tagging Related Issues:** Users can tag related issues in their saved view, making it simple to find the connections and history of these issues.

**4. Filtering and Sorting Options:** Users can filter and sort issues in their saved view based on various criteria such as severity, type, and priority, making it easier to find relevant information quickly.

**5. Customizable View Layout:** Users can customize the layout of their saved view to suit their preferences, ensuring that the information is presented in a way that is most helpful to them.

## FAQ

**1. Accessing Private Views:** To access your private saved view, simply log in to your GitHub account, go to the repository you want to view, and click on the "Saved Views" tab. From there, you can select your private view and view the issues.

**2. Sharing Views:** If you want to share your private saved view with others, you can click on the "Share" button next to the view you want to share. You can choose to share with a specific group of people or the entire repository.

**3. Regularly Update Views:** To keep your private saved view up-to-date, make sure to periodically update the issues within the repository. This will ensure that the information displayed in your view is always accurate and relevant to your workflow.
